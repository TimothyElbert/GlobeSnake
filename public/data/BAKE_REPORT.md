## What is real vs. synthesized

- **Land/ocean mask**: Natural Earth 50m land polygons
- **Land elevation**: NASA GEBCO_08 rev elev (mean+max block downsample). The PNG's grey encoding is nonlinear; calibrated piecewise against known elevations (≈18 m/grey up to grey 180 ≈ 3240 m, then ≈74.8 m/grey with grey 255 anchored to Everest 8848 m). Mid-mountain elevations (3–5 km) may read up to ~20% high.
- **Bathymetry**: SYNTHESIZED everywhere — smooth-stepped distance-to-coast to a ~5400–6100 m abyssal plain plus seeded noise. No real bathymetric grid is ingested; the elevation byte over ocean is plausible, not measured.
- **Climate**: Beck et al. Köppen-Geiger V1 present-day 0.5° (CC BY 4.0), procedural infill for gaps. 100.0% of land texels carry a real class; the remainder (small islands / coastal slivers below 0.5° resolution) use the procedural classifier.
- **Countries**: world-atlas countries-50m (Natural Earth ids).
- **Terrain**: derived (classification over climate + elevation + slope + NE water/glacier/river layers).

## Classification rules (defensibility notes)

- Mountain requires elevation AND steepness, measured in the GEBCO grey-linear domain to avoid stretch-amplified plateau jitter: slope≥40 m/km @ ≥600 m, ≥30 @ ≥1200 m, ≥20 @ ≥2500 m, or ruggedness (max slope within ±2 texels) ≥50 @ ≥1000 m. The ruggedness tier fills range interiors so the Andes/Rockies/Alps read as continuous ranges, tuned to ≈10% of land texels. Flat plateau centres (Tibet) fail all tiers and stay plains; plateau margins classify as mountain.
- Desert = Köppen BW. **Deviation from the brief**: BS (steppe) maps to plains, not desert — steppe is grassland; this keeps the Great Plains, Sahel and Kazakh steppe traversable grassland rather than desert.
- Forest = Af, Am, Cf*, Cw*, Df*, Dw* (Dw = East Siberian taiga; omitting it would leave half of Siberia unforested). **Deviation**: Aw (savanna) maps to plains, not forest.
- Ice = EF, or Natural Earth glaciated areas, or ET at |lat| ≥ 66° (Arctic tundra). Alpine/subarctic ET (Tibet, the Scandes, Iceland lowlands) is NOT ice. Mountain overrides ice on steep terrain so the Himalayan front reads mountain, while flat ice sheets (central Greenland, Antarctica) read ice.
- Coast = land texel with water in its 8-neighbourhood. Shallow = ocean within ~2 texels of land. River = NE 50m river centerlines (1 texel wide, land only, drawn last). Lake = NE 50m lakes.
- The Caspian Sea is outside the NE land polygons and therefore reads as **ocean** (terrain 0/1, country 0, synthesized depth); Lake Victoria, the Great Lakes, Baikal etc. read as lake (9).
- Country outline SVGs: per-country equirectangular ×cos(mean lat), no rotation normalisation; subpaths <0.3% of country area dropped; far-flung dependencies (sea gap >35% of the mainland span, e.g. Svalbard for Norway, Azores/Madeira for Portugal) excluded so mainland silhouettes stay identifiable.
